The order page — reached it, didn't buy

"Reached it" is once per browser per day, same as a visit. "Left it" is a real moment: she moved on from the order page, within the same visit, without an order going through — it misses anyone who simply closed the tab instead. "Didn't complete" is a wider estimate that catches that too: reached it, minus however many orders were actually placed that day — a same-day sum, not a trace of any one visitor; it can run a little low or high on a quiet day, and never below zero.
